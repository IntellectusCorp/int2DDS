> This Trademark Policy describes the permitted and prohibited uses of
> trademarks associated with the **int2DDS** project.
> The purpose of this policy is to protect the integrity of the project,
> avoid user confusion, and preserve the value of the int2DDS brand.

## 3. Permitted Uses

> You may use the int2DDS trademarks **without prior written permission**
> in the following cases:
>
> - To refer to the unmodified int2DDS project accurately and truthfully
> - To state that your software is _compatible with_ or _based on_ int2DDS,
>   provided that such use does not imply endorsement or certification
> - In documentation, academic papers, presentations, or discussions
>   that reference the project factually

---

## 4. Prohibited Uses

> You may **not** use the int2DDS trademarks in the following ways
> without explicit written permission from Intellectus Corp.:
>
> - Using "int2DDS" or "int2ConneX" in the name of a forked or derived project
> - Using the trademarks in a way that implies:
>   - official status
>   - endorsement
>   - certification
>   - partnership
> - Using logos or brand elements in commercial products,
>   marketing materials, or services
> - Registering domain names, product names, or company names
>   that include the int2DDS trademarks

---

## 5. Forks and Derived Works

> Forks and derived works of the int2DDS codebase
> must use a **distinct name** and must not use the int2DDS trademarks
> in a manner that causes confusion.
>
> Acceptable examples:
>
> - "XYZ DDS (based on int2DDS)"
> - "ABC Middleware, compatible with int2DDS"
>
> Unacceptable examples:
>
> - "int2DDS Plus"
> - "int2DDS Enterprise"
> - "Official int2DDS Fork"
